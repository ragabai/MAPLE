## Limitations (automatically collected)

- Pretraining used ~15k steps of a ~4M-parameter PFN on shared GPUs; production tabular foundation models train orders of magnitude longer. Conclusions concern the controlled equal-budget comparison.
- Real datasets with more than 32 features are reduced by train-only mutual-information selection for the PFN (not for the baselines); training sets above 1024 rows use context bagging.
- Ablation and sensitivity runs use a single pretraining seed.
- Noise level in the generator is independent of the cell, which limits the cell-level advantage that Prop. 4(a) predicts for disagreement over loss scores (pilot report).
